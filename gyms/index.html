Questions gym owners ask

I already have a website.

Same packages and prices. I rebuild it so it works on phones and books trials, your old site stays up while you review the new one on a private link, and we switch over on your domain once you approve it.

What counts as a round of changes?

You send me one list of everything you want different (words, photos, colours, order) and I make all of it. Starter includes two rounds, Gym Growth three. Fixing a mistake of mine never counts as a round.

What about changes after it's live?

On the care plan, just text or email me and it's done within 48 hours. Without it, small changes are quoted before I start, and you can join or leave the care plan any time.

Can it work with my booking software?

Usually. Many gym booking and membership tools have a booking link or widget I can put on the site. Connecting deeper than that is part of the Custom package, and I'll check what yours allows on the call.

Are there monthly fees?

None required. The hosting I set up is free and your domain is about $12 a year. The $50/mo care plan is optional, for gyms that would rather text me changes than do them.

Who writes it, and what about photos?

I write it from the call and you check that it sounds like you. Phone photos and videos of real classes beat stock photos for a gym, and I'll tell you exactly which shots to take.

You're not local. Does that matter?

No. I work with gyms anywhere in the US over video call and email, and you see every step as a working link.

Do I have to get on a call?

No. The call is the fastest way to get it right, but everything can be done by email.
