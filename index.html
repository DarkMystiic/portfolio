Questions owners ask

Do I need to buy a domain name?

No. Your site can go live on a free address. A name of your own, like thirdchairbarbers.com, costs about $12 a year from any domain seller, and I'll connect it for you.

I already have a website.

Same packages and prices. I rebuild it on the new design, your old site stays up while you review the new one on a private link, and we switch over on your domain once you approve it.

Which package do I need?

If people mostly need to see what you do and how to reach you, Starter. If you want them to book or request a quote from the site, Growth. If you need payments or software connected, Custom. Not sure? Ask, and I'll tell you honestly, including when the cheaper one is enough.

What counts as a round of changes?

You send me one list of everything you want different (words, photos, colours, order) and I make all of it. Starter includes two rounds, Growth three. Fixing a mistake of mine never counts as a round.

Are there monthly fees?

None required. The hosting I set up is free and your domain is about $12 a year. If you'd rather text me changes than make them yourself, there's an optional $50/mo care plan.

Who writes the words?

I do, from what you tell me about your business. You read it, and anything that doesn't sound like you gets changed.

I don't have good photos.

Phone photos of your work are usually enough, and I'll pick the best. Where there are gaps, I use licensed stock photos that fit.

Can I change things later?

Yes. Rounds of changes are included before launch. After that, the care plan covers changes, or I quote small changes before I start.

How long does it take?

Starter is live in about a week and Growth in about two, depending on how fast we go back and forth. Custom gets a timeline with its quote.
