Questions owners ask

Do I need to buy a domain name?

No. Your site can go live on a free address. A name of your own, like thirdchairbarbers.com, costs about $12 a year from any domain seller, and I'll connect it for you.

Are there monthly fees?

Not from me, and the hosting I set up is free. The only ongoing cost is your domain, if you choose to have one.

Who writes the words?

I do, from our 15-minute chat. You read it, and anything that doesn't sound like you gets changed.

I don't have good photos.

Phone photos of your work are usually enough, and I'll pick the best. Where there are gaps, I use licensed stock photos that fit.

Can I change things later?

Yes. Two rounds of changes are included before launch, and after that you can text me when your prices or hours change.

How long does it take?

A first version within a few days and live in about a week, depending on how fast we go back and forth.
