# Portfolio v2: design brief (2026-11-01)

**Job:** a Bay Area business owner texts or emails Jaylen about a website.
**Reader:** an owner of a barbershop, gym, cleaning company, landscaping business or trade. They have no website (or a bad one). Most found Jaylen from a text he sent them, so they read it on a phone, between jobs, in under a minute.
**5-second test:** what = websites; for whom = Bay Area local businesses; action = see the samples / get in touch.
**Offer:** $150 one-page site (tiers later, not yet).
**Contact:** email only (form + direct address); no texting or calls for now (owner, 2026-11-01). Name + city, no photo.

## Look: warm editorial, upgraded
- Keep the brand: paper `#f6f2ea`, card `#fffdf8`, ink `#17140f`, muted `#6b645a`, line `#e2dbcf`, one accent `#e4572e`. Fraunces for headlines, Inter for reading.
- **The one memorable thing:** a real phone in the hero that runs the three sample sites live. Visitors switch between Barber, Gym and Cleaning and scroll the actual site with their finger. It's proof and demo in one.
- Everything around it stays quiet: no fade-in on every section, no arrow on every button, no all-caps label above every heading, no numbered markers unless it's a real sequence (only the 3-step process).

## Page order
1. Hero: headline + one line on who it's for, [See the samples] [Ask me a question], the live phone.
2. "Search your business name": what customers find today (a map pin, no website) vs with a site.
3. Three sample sites, one per row: what each is built to do and a link to open it.
4. CombatLab: the real shipped project.
5. The $150 offer: exactly what's included.
6. How it works (3 steps).
7. Questions owners ask (domain, hosting cost, words, photos, edits).
8. Contact: email form + direct email.

## Fight-gym page (`gyms/`, 2026-11-01)
**Job:** a fight-gym owner books a 20-minute call (email form; no booking tool yet).
**Reader:** owner of a boxing, Muay Thai or MMA gym anywhere in the US (owner: clients "anywhere"). Reads it from an email or DM link, on a phone.
**5-second test:** what = websites that fill free-trial classes; for whom = fight gyms; action = book a call.
**Offer:** Starter $300 · Gym Growth $1,200 (recommended) · Custom from $3,000 · optional care plan $50/mo. 50% to start, 50% at launch.
**Memorable element:** "Member math": the owner's own membership price, retention and trial numbers → what one member is worth and how many new members pay for the site. Labelled as their numbers, not a promise.
**Proof:** the Fogline sample live in the phone, CombatLab, and Jaylen's own love of boxing and Muay Thai.
**Order:** hero + phone → where trials get lost (unanswered DM vs a booked class) → packages → member math → why me → how it works → questions → contact.
